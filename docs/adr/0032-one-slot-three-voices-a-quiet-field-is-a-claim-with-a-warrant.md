# 0032: One slot, three voices — a quiet field is a claim with a warrant, and a Scout that is not looking says so

**Status:** Accepted

`CLAUDE.md` § Invariants: *"a broken Scout must never look identical to a quiet field."* Question #313 found that the frozen prompts ask for the broken half twice (Prompt 8 — Home, Prompt 10 — Scout Activity) and never for the half it contrasts against, so both pinned prototypes carry the distinction as **copy asserting it** — `08-home.html`'s *"A scout that has quietly stopped parsing looks exactly like a quiet field"* and `10-scout-activity.html`'s *"Twenty-seven runs have each reported '0 new'"* — inside the broken Scout's own row, with nothing drawn beside it. A reader of the PNGs has to take the contrast on trust.

Meanwhile prototype `12-empty-vault.html` (pinned 2026-09-28, #314) designed a general mechanism for exactly this class of problem and declared it reusable — *"Learn them on the Inbox and they read the same on any surface drawn after it"* — and it is in no living document. This ADR, from a `grill-with-docs` pass on 2026-09-28, lifts that mechanism into the vocabulary, applies it to Scout health, and settles the treatment once so that beats 6, 7, 9 and 12 cannot each invent one. The vocabulary is `CONTEXT.md` § The app and § Scouts; the derivation and the tables are `docs/architecture.md` § Scouts.

The hinge is prototype 12's own sentence: **only one of the three voices asserts anything, so it is the only one that shows what the assertion rests on.** The brief's dangerous claim — a Scout that stopped finding things "looks identical to a quiet field" — is true only while *nothing new* is a zero in a column. It stops being true when *nothing new* is a sentence that must carry its warrant.

## Decisions

1. **Three voices, and they are the app's, not the Inbox's.** Every surface that can be short has a first slot where its contents would begin, and what sits there is one of exactly three things. **Claim**: it looked, and there is nothing — a full sentence, in the interface face, with a full stop, carrying a Warrant. **Not yet**: it has not looked — a mono fragment, no full stop, no Warrant, optionally with the reason it has not. **Wrong**: it tried to look and could not — the same fragment shape with the warning glyph and a reason, never red, never dismissible, gone as soon as it stops being true. The rule binds every surface, not only the ones drawn so far: beat 5 (Reader), beat 11 (Vault), the Experiment inbox and anything after them inherit it rather than each inventing an empty state. Recording it as merely available was rejected — that is how eleven surfaces end up with eleven empty states, which is what prototype 12 was drawn to stop.
2. **Only a claim carries a Warrant, and the Warrant sits where provenance sits.** A Warrant is what the assertion rests on, in the slot on the row that otherwise carries provenance. *Not yet* and *wrong* have none, because neither asserts anything about the world; giving them one would be claiming to have proved something nobody looked for. This is the single test that separates the voices, and the reason the distinction survives a careless render: a row asserting *nothing is there* with an empty provenance slot is visibly unfinished.
3. **A reason is not a fault, and which voice carries it says which.** *Not yet* may carry a reason, and it is always **why it has not looked** — never a failure. *Wrong* always carries one, and it is always **why it could not** — always a failure. Prototype 12 gave *not yet* no reason slot because a vault being read has only one reason; a Scout has several, so the slot is added here. The marks that separate the two at a glance are the glyph and the absence of warm colour.
4. **A quiet field is a claim, and its Warrant is three fragments.** A Scout that ran, parsed cleanly and found nothing says so as a sentence — *"Nothing new since 14 September."* — warranted by three facts, each defeating a different confusion: **when it last ran** (against *stale*), **that the run parsed cleanly** (against *broken*), and **what this source usually yields** (against a reliably productive source gone quiet). All three show always. A Scout with too little history says *no baseline yet* rather than dropping the fragment: a missing fragment would make a young Scout look like a hiding one. Showing the baseline only when it contradicts was rejected — that is a suspicion threshold reintroduced as a rendering rule.
5. **"A reliably productive source gone unusually quiet" needs no state; the Warrant carries it.** The brief lists it under *structure change detected* and ADR 0016 decision 10 deferred it to beat 9 for want of run history to compare against. Decision 4 discharges the deferral instead of satisfying it: `ran 6h ago · parsed cleanly · nothing new in 6 weeks · usually ~4 a week` is a claim that visibly undercuts itself, and `…usually ~1 a quarter` is the same shape and obviously fine. No threshold, no flag, and no amber state sitting between *fine* and *broken* — which is the mush this invariant exists to prevent. The comparison is the reader's, because the app cannot defend a number for how quiet is too quiet.
6. **Fields arriving systematically empty is not a health state.** Prototype 10 draws `sleep-proceedings` as `stale` / *"stale extraction"* / *"partial extraction"*. It is none of those: its items verify (a title and link occurring literally on the page — `CONTEXT.md` § Verified — is all verification asks), so the run is plain `ok` by `docs/architecture.md` § Scouts, Outcomes; and its cadence is monthly and it ran 17 days ago, so it is not overdue either. It is a **healthy Scout whose accept rate is a lie**. That is a property of one derived number, not of the Scout, so the accept-rate cell states why it cannot be computed and health stays `ok`. *Stale* keeps `CONTEXT.md`'s narrow meaning — a last successful run that is old — and is not widened. A fourth health state was rejected: it would carry a fact about the measure on the slot reserved for the Scout, which is exactly what let the prototype imply a working Scout was half-broken. The Loose Ends row stays, because a degraded extractor is fixable and Loose Ends is what is fixable.
7. **One fault sentence, derived once, identical on every surface.** The same broken Scout is described three times in three pinned prototypes in three different sentences — Home's `what`, Loose Ends' `title` and `why`, Scout Activity's `diagnosisText`. That is the brief's *"two half-versions of the same list"* (§ Loose Ends, Overlap with Home), tripled. ADR 0016 decision 10 already single-sources the *derivation* so the surfaces cannot disagree; this extends it to the *sentence*. One statement per error kind, derived in the core from the run row and rendered verbatim wherever the fault appears. Surfaces differ only in their actions and in whether they show it at all. **One carve-out:** Scout Activity may add a second sentence about the measure — that the accept rate above is meaningless while the fault holds — because that is its own question and appears nowhere else. A second sentence, never a rewrite of the first.
8. **Home claims coverage of looking, not absence of failure.** Prototype 08's quiet-morning opener is *"Nothing is broken."* Under decision 1 that is a claim owing a Warrant, and writing the Warrant exposes that it is the wrong claim: *nothing is broken* is true while three Scouts are paused and a fourth has no key, and from the researcher's side those produce **exactly the same silence as a broken one**. So Home makes one aggregate claim, and it is about whether the field is being watched — *"Twelve scouts watching. Two are not: `density-watch`, `sleep-proceedings`."* — warranted by the fleet's liveness. The ones that are not looking are **named, never counted**, reusing ADR 0025 decision 3's device for Scouts blocked on a key so that Home and Settings say it the same way. Broken keeps its interrupt block above this, unchanged. Home carries no per-Scout claim: it is light by design, and twelve quiet sentences is not a landing.
9. **Scout Activity counts faults; quiet is never counted.** Prototype 10's `10 parsing cleanly · 1 stale · 1 broken` becomes counts by fault only — `10 parsing cleanly · 1 not parsing · 1 no key`. The ten include every quiet Scout, because quiet is not a state but a healthy Scout with nothing to report, and its evidence is its own row's Warrant. A *quiet* count was rejected: it fails the standing rule that a count counts what exists and never what is owed, and it would re-create the debt framing around Scouts that are working correctly — the pinned art's mistake in reverse.
10. **This binds beats 6, 7, 9 and 12, and their spec grills do not reopen it.** #313 names beats 9 (#176) and 12 (#182); that undercounts. Beat 6 (#173) ships the health derivation and the first *Broken plumbing* row (ADR 0016 decision 12) and comes first of the four; beat 7 (#174) adds *blocked on credentials* to the broken-Scouts count and *structure change detected* as a row (`docs/architecture.md` § Scouts, Watched sources). The voice assignment and the Warrant's contents are out of reach of all four spec grills, on the terms ADR 0016 decision 6 set for its own list; a later beat's grill may reopen them. Without this, beat 6 specs a health row three beats before Scout Activity exists and the two disagree — the failure this ADR is named after.
11. **The shipped Inbox and Loose Ends empty states predate the mechanism and owe conformance.** `packages/renderer/src/LooseEnds.tsx` still says *"Nothing to tidy that the app can see yet"*; prototype 12 removed *yet* deliberately — it *"sounded like a promise"* — and moved the honest-empty weight into the Warrant. `packages/renderer/src/Inbox.tsx` has the right instinct (it suppresses the count on a failed read, so a failure cannot read as an empty vault) and none of the mechanism: no Warrant on the empty state, no glyphs, and a failure carried on `role="alert"`, which is assertive and at odds with *a failure is stated in the same quiet voice as an ordinary fact*. The drift is named here and ticketed rather than fixed on this branch: `CLAUDE.md` starts no implementation without a spec issue, and the `role="alert"` question wants a test rather than a drive-by edit.

## Considered options

- **A prototype rather than an ADR** (#313 offered either). Rejected once prototype 12 landed: the treatment is already drawn, frozen and declared reusable, so drawing a Scout-specific contrast set would mint a *second* vocabulary — the failure prototype 12 exists to prevent. The objection #313 raises against the ADR route (that prose asserting a contrast is not a design) does not apply to an ADR that *applies* a drawn mechanism rather than inventing one.
- **Putting all of this in ADR 0016.** Rejected, decision 1: the three voices bind eleven surfaces, and burying that inside an ADR titled *the first Scout beat* hides a general rule inside a decision about arXiv. ADR 0016 takes a dated update for the part that is genuinely its own.
- **A suspicion threshold for "unusually quiet".** Rejected, decision 5.
- **A fourth health state for systematically empty fields.** Rejected, decision 6.
- **Three surface-specific fault sentences.** Rejected, decision 7: three texts about one fault is how the surfaces drift apart, and single-sourcing the query while leaving the sentence free only defers the disagreement to the first edit.
- **Keeping Home's "Nothing is broken."** Rejected, decision 8: true, and materially misleading, which is #313's own defect committed by the surface the brief calls the most important alert in the app.
- **Counting quiet Scouts on Scout Activity.** Rejected, decision 9.
- **Pushing *blocked on credentials* into the *wrong* voice.** Rejected, decision 3: it contradicts `CONTEXT.md` § Blocked on credentials (*"Nothing has failed; something is missing"*) and ADR 0025, and would put a warning mark on a Scout the user has simply not finished setting up.
- **Re-pinning prototypes 08 and 10.** Rejected: it costs a human Claude Design session and owes the `tacomancy/tacomancy` issue `CLAUDE.md` § The public site describes, and buys no design this ADR does not state. The art was right about the fault; it lacked the other half of the pair. The moves are recorded below instead, as ADR 0016's update of 2026-09-26 did for prototypes 06 and 11.
- **Fixing the shipped empty states on this branch.** Rejected, decision 11.

## Consequences

- *Nothing new* stops being a number anywhere in the app. Wherever a Scout reports it, it is a sentence with a Warrant, and a Scout that cannot warrant it cannot say it.
- The three surfaces' agreement is now structural in two places rather than one: the run-row query (ADR 0016 decision 10) and the fault sentence derived from it (decision 7). A surface can still choose *whether* to show a fault; it cannot restate one.
- Beat 9 loses a deferred item (decision 5) rather than gaining one, and gains the accept-rate caveat as a first-class thing to render (decisions 6 and 7).
- *Not yet* is now a voice with a reason, which is close in shape to *wrong* with a reason: two marks separate them (the glyph, and warm colour) where prototype 12's Inbox trio had four. If that proves too thin in the drawing, the remedy is a distinct glyph for *blocked on credentials*, not a change to the voices.
- Every surface built after this inherits an empty-state treatment instead of choosing one, which is a constraint on beats 5, 11 and the Experiment inbox that none of their beat issues currently names.

## What this moves in the prototypes

The frozen tier is never edited (`docs/reference/README.md`); these are recorded so a reader of the PNGs knows which lines the decision has overtaken.

- **`08-home.html`** — the quiet-morning opener *"Nothing is broken."* becomes a claim about coverage of looking, with a Warrant and the not-looking Scouts named (decision 8). The broken block's closing line, *"A scout that has quietly stopped parsing looks exactly like a quiet field. This is the only thing on Home that will interrupt you,"* loses its first sentence: the row no longer needs telling, because the quiet Scouts on the same screen are making warranted claims and the broken one is not. The second sentence stands.
- **`10-scout-activity.html`** — `health: 'stale'` and the labels *"stale extraction"* / *"partial extraction"* are withdrawn (decision 6); `sleep-proceedings` is a healthy Scout whose accept-rate cell states why it cannot be computed. The summary line drops its *stale* count and never gains a *quiet* one (decision 9). The diagnosis sentence *"Twenty-seven runs have each reported '0 new', which is indistinguishable from a quiet field until you look here"* is overtaken by the eleven rows above it, each carrying a Warrant the broken row cannot; its second half — *"The accept rate above is meaningless while this is true"* — is exactly decision 7's carve-out and stands. Every row gains a Warrant; `last` alone was never enough, since it names the last **run** and not the last **new item**, which is the datum the whole distinction turns on.
- **`09-loose-ends.html`** — unchanged. Its `sleep-proceedings` row stays under *Broken plumbing*: a degraded extractor is fixable, which is what that dashboard is for, and decision 6 only removes it from the Scout's *health*.
- **`12-empty-vault.html`** — unchanged and load-bearing. Decision 3 adds a reason slot to its *not yet* voice, which the Inbox never needed.

## Update (2026-10-07, ADR 0043)

**ADR 0043** narrows decision 4's Warrant. For a Watched Scout all three fragments — when it last ran, that the run parsed cleanly, and what the source usually yields — are read from the runs at its current page, so a Scout pointed at another page is *not yet* until a run has read it, and a Quiet field's rate says *no baseline yet* until three clean runs have. The three Voices and the rule that only a claim carries a Warrant are unchanged.
