# 0055: A Watched Scout reads each new item's own page, declared metadata first, and a Paper link is shown but never an identity

**Status:** Accepted

Test-driving the two Watched Scouts on Anthropic's team pages — the brief's own first Scout example (`## Scouts`, *Anthropic Alignment output · Lab publications page*) — gave 46 Proposals with no authors, no venue, no date and mostly no abstract. The dates were a reduction bug (#881, PR #882). The rest is not on the listing at all: it is on each item's own page, which ADR 0017 decision 5 and ADR 0040 decision 3 forbid a Scout to fetch ("no link-following"). A grill on 2026-10-27 settled what a Scout may read beyond its page, how, and what it may do with a link to a paper. The vocabulary is `CONTEXT.md` § Scouts — *Watched source*, *Item page*, *Verified*, *Listing-only*, *Paper link*; the shape is `docs/architecture.md` § BYOK and watched sources.

## Decisions

1. **Whether an item is a paper or a post is never decided.** The brief says "a blog post with no venue or keyword list is still a valid card" and that an unfiltered lab Scout "will legitimately produce both kinds"; Lanes and Mute rules govern attention. *Agentic misalignment* is a paper published as a web page, and no link-based rule can tell it from a post. The problem was empty cards, and the fix is the fields.
2. **A Watched Scout reads the Item page of each item it has not seen, on the listing's host, up to 25 per run; nothing linked from an Item page is followed.** ADR 0017 decision 5's "one fetch… no link-following" and ADR 0040 decision 3's "no link-following, no crawl" become *one page, its feed, and the Item pages of new items on the same host*. robots.txt, the user agent, the 30 s timeout and the 2 MB cap apply to each. An Item page's reduced text has its own budget of **8K** input tokens, since the card fields sit at the top of a page that may be a whole paper.
3. **Declared metadata first, an Extraction for the gap.** What the Item page declares for machines — `citation_*`, JSON-LD, OpenGraph — fills the card with no model call. An Extraction of that page is made only for fields still empty. Same rule as ADR 0040 decision 3: what is declared is read, never guessed at, and the paid path is only for what is missing.
4. **Verified means each copied value occurs literally on the page it was copied from.** On a listing that is still the title and link, and an item that fails is dropped. On an Item page the title and link are already known, so a model's mistakes would land in the authors, date or abstract instead: every field the Extraction filled is checked, a value that fails is dropped and counted, and the rest of the card stands. A date is checked as copied (`Jun 20, 2025`) and formatted after.
5. **Past the ceiling, a card is Listing-only, and it fills in later.** Items beyond the 25 are proposed at once from what the listing showed and read on later runs: this run's new items first, then Listing-only cards oldest first. When its Item page is read, a pending card gains the fields it lacked. Only empty fields are filled, never one already set, and a triaged card is never touched. This is the brief's "most complete metadata winning" (`## Scouts` § Deduplication), and it also covers the cards already pending from before this ADR. The page-unchanged short-circuit (ADR 0043 d.5) skips only the listing's Extraction; Listing-only cards are still read.
6. **An Item page that fails is counted, retried, and a health signal only in bulk.** The card stays Listing-only and the run counts it ("3 item pages unread"). A failed Item page is tried again on later runs, at most three times in all. If fewer than half of a run's Item page reads succeed, the run records `extraction` with message *item pages unread*. Its listing's cards are still proposed: *last new item* advances, but *last successful extraction* does not. A site that starts refusing these reads then looks broken, not quiet (`## Scouts` § Source health), and one dead link breaks nothing.
7. **A Feed entry's Item page is read for declared metadata only, never by a model.** A feed Scout stays free, and the Feed path never quietly turns into a paid one (ADR 0040 decision 3).
8. **Try shows what a first look will cost.** *Try* reads the listing and up to three Item pages, shows what those cost, and projects the first look (*≈ $X for 24 item pages*). Cost is still shown where it is chosen and nowhere else, and there is still no cap (ADR 0040 decision 6).
9. **Authors are people.** An organisation the page names as its author (JSON-LD `Organization`) never fills *authors*: Rank's first signal is author overlap, and one lab as everyone's author would make every card from it match every other. Declared person authors are used; otherwise the Extraction copies names from the byline or citation block, each Verified. A site's own undocumented author fields are not read. On anthropic.com that field is empty on three of four pages and wrong on the fourth.
10. **A Paper link is shown and carried, never an identity.** The Extraction may copy the link the Item page's own text gives as this work's paper (*read the paper*, *full paper*), Verified against the page, and null when it names none. The card shows it beside the link back, and an accepted stub carries it as `paper:` beside `url:`, which stays the Item page. It is never the Source key, and `paper:` is never a key a stub answers to for Held. An Item page also links works it only cites — *Agentic misalignment* links two cited arXiv papers and no paper of its own — so a wrong pick is possible, and as a link it can be ignored. As an identity it would merge two works, or Hold a real card out of the Queue, with nothing on screen to show it.
11. **A listing is what it serves.** A Watched source's Retroactive batch is the items in the served HTML, not what a *see more* control would load (the alignment page serves 24 of the 48 it advertises). New items appear at the top, so ongoing runs miss nothing, and the back catalogue is a Structured source's Retroactive search to make.

## Considered options

- **Classifying paper against post, by link or by model; labelling the card; holding posts back.** Rejected, decision 1: a filter the brief rules out everywhere ("ranking is a default sort, never a filter"), and a rule the evidence shows cannot be written from the link.
- **Following any link to any depth; following an Item page's arXiv or DOI links.** Rejected, decisions 2 and 10. The first is a crawl, and the second attaches cited works.
- **Always an Extraction per Item page; declared metadata only.** Rejected, decision 3. The first pays for what is declared free, and the second leaves authors empty on every anthropic.com page.
- **Checking only the title on an Item page; looser checks for authors and dates.** Rejected, decision 4. The fields left unchecked are the ones a model would get wrong.
- **No carry-over past the ceiling; withholding a card until its Item page is read.** Rejected, decision 5. The first leaves cards empty for good, and the second delays a monthly Scout's new finds by a month.
- **Failing the run on any Item page failure; never letting failures count toward health.** Rejected, decision 6: one cries wolf, the other lets a refusing site look like a quiet field.
- **A confirm step before a first look.** Rejected, decision 8: a gate the brief does not ask for, where a projection on the form already gives the number.
- **The organisation as author; a BibTeX parser.** Rejected, decision 9. The first floods Rank. The second is a second parser for what the Extraction already copies, with no second use to justify it.
- **A Paper link as the Source key, or as a Held key; `url:` replaced by it.** Rejected, decision 10. The last loses where the work was found.
- **A pointer between an announcement card and its paper's own card; merging them.** Not decided this beat. The brief's "lab announcement + preprint" merge cannot happen by Source key, and a pointer is a Queue change that wants its own pass against the prototype. Reopen when a Paper link and its paper's own card both reach Review.
- **Reading a sitemap, or detecting a *see more* control.** Rejected, decision 11. The first is a crawl by another name, and the second is a guess from button text that would itself be a false signal.

## Consequences

- **+** The brief's first Scout example produces cards with dates, summaries and, where the page has them, authors and a link to the paper.
- **+** A feed Scout is still free, and nothing a model chose decides which work a card is.
- **−** A first look can cost roughly 15–20× a listing read (≈ $1–1.50 for 25 Item pages on `claude-opus-5`, against $0.07 for the listing). It is shown on *try* and on the run, and is uncapped.
- **−** A Proposal's fields can now change while it is pending, filling only. The Queue's detail must show the card as it is now, never a snapshot taken when it arrived.
- **−** An announcement and its paper found by an arXiv Scout stay two cards.

## Update (2026-10-28, ADR 0056)

**ADR 0056** applies decision 2's ceiling of 25 Item pages and decision 8's *try* per page: a check of several pages reads up to 25 for each, and *try* reads up to three per page and sums their projections. An item two of a Scout's pages list is one card, and its Item page is read once.
