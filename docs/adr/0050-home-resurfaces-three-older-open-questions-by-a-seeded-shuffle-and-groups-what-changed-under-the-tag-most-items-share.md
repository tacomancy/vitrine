# 0050: Home resurfaces three older open Questions by a seeded shuffle, and groups what changed under the Tag most items share

**Status:** Accepted

Spec #728 (beat #182), tickets #732–#734. Resolves the fog item on map #131, *Home's resurfacing mechanism*, which Prompt 8 settled by tone (*"opening a notebook at a random page"*) and left the mechanism to this beat. It also records the rules for *changed while you were away*, the one other place Home derives something no earlier surface did. Everything else on Home renders a derivation that already existed (ADR 0016, 0032, 0042, 0044) and is not decided again here.

## Decisions

1. **The pool is open Questions at least 60 days old.** A Question with status `open` (a file with no `status:` is open: ADR 0009), captured at least `RESURFACE_AFTER_DAYS` = 60 calendar days before today, not dismissed from the rotation. A promoted, answered or abandoned Question leaves by itself because the pool is derived from the status on every read, never stored. A Partial and an Unreadable file are not listed Questions, so neither can be picked. Age here is a calendar fact, not a quiet period, so open days are not involved (§ Loose Ends) and resurfacing creates no row and no debt.
2. **Three, by a shuffle seeded by the local date plus a client seed.** The pool is sorted by path, then Fisher–Yates shuffled with a mulberry32 stream seeded from a hash of `<local date>:<seed>`. The same date and seed give the same three on every read and every machine: a reload does not reshuffle, tomorrow is a new page, and *↻ others* (seed + 1) draws another three from the same pool. The mechanism is predictable; the tone is random. Fewer than three in the pool shows what exists; an empty pool draws no section.
3. **Nothing weights the pick.** Not age, Tag, link count or Scout match. Any signal would turn a random page into a ranking, and nothing in the brief asks for one. Considered: weighting by age — it would put the oldest Questions first every time, which is a to-do list sorted by neglect.
4. **The reply carries no count of the pool.** *How many older Questions there are* is not Home's to say; a number there would be the debt the brief's *No expiry and no nagging* rules out.
5. **A dismissal leaves the rotation, never the vault.** `home.dismissResurfaced` writes `.vitrine/dismissals.json` under the row kind `resurfaced`, keyed by the Question's `id` (its path when it has none), through the same store as Loose Ends (ADR 0016). The kind is judged apart from every Loose Ends kind, so a `resurfaced` dismissal silences no Loose Ends row and a Loose Ends one does not remove a Question from the rotation. Nothing in the Question's file is touched. *Undo* (`home.undismissResurfaced`) stays beside the line while it is on screen and is gone on the next read, as Loose Ends' is (#266). A dismissal is permanent unless undone: Home holds no Setting (ADR 0025 decision 2), so there is no *un-dismiss everything*. The two procedures are Home's own rather than a widening of the Loose Ends pair, so neither contract moves.
6. **The changed window opens at the start of the previous open day.** The latest `open_days` date strictly before today (`OpenDays.before`, beside `since`; read-only, and nothing that counts a quiet period), so a Monday after a week away reaches back to the previous open day and not to yesterday. With no previous open day the window is the last 7 days; it is capped at 14, so a month away reads as a fortnight. It runs to now: a future-dated timestamp is no event yet. `AppOptions.now` is the clock.
7. **An item needs a timestamp the vault already records.** A structured Kind is never reported changed on its file `mtime`: that moves under sync, a restore and Obsidian's own rewrites, and would put a claim on the page with no warrant. Shipped, each from its own date: a Question *captured* (`captured:`); a Position Revision (the entry's `at`, named by its field: *Working answer revised*, *Claim revised*, or *Criterion edited after Evidence*); a source stub *accepted* from a Scout (the `triage` accept row); an Experiment *run recorded* (`created:`). A plain **Note** has no event record, so its `mtime` in the window is its one item, *Note edited*; this is a deliberate narrowing of the brief's *recent vault activity*.
8. **Omitted for want of a timestamp, and recorded as omitted.** A Source's *highlights ingested*: Ingest records no time of its own (the sidecar's `last_matched` moves on every re-match, the PDF's `mtime` is a file time). The prototype's *Hypothesis falsified* has no criterion timestamp either. Neither is filled from `mtime`; a test pins that a touched Source yields no item.
9. **The topic is the Tag, and each item lands once.** An item's topic is the Tag, among its own, that the most other items in the window also carry, ties broken alphabetically, so the page offers fewer, fuller threads and the same vault always groups the same. A Tag is grouped as written, at its own depth, with no roll-up of `ml/interpretability/probing` into `ml`: the depth control belongs to the Question Map, where double-counting matters, and Home counts nothing. A Question or Source whose Tags are `invalid` is grouped by the reason, never dropped. Items with no Tag go under one final group named *untagged*.
10. **As built, a Hypothesis's or Experiment's topic is its own Tags.** The spec has them take the Tags of the Question they descend from; that was narrowed in #734, so an untagged one lands in *untagged*. The descent is still the intended rule and the gap is recorded here rather than closed silently.
11. **Shape: five groups of three, and no total.** Groups ordered by their newest item (untagged last, and it keeps its slot when the cap bites, so an item is never dropped for lacking a Tag); items newest first, ties by path. The remainder is not counted or hinted at: the group simply ends. The reply carries the window's start (`since`) because an empty section's Warrant says what was looked at and since when.
12. **A failed read is a *wrong* voice with its reason.** For `home.changed` and `home.resurfaced` alike, never an empty section (ADR 0032, 0033). A section with nothing to say and no claim to make is not drawn.

## Considered options

- **Weight the rotation by age, Tag or Scout match** — rejected for decision 3.
- **Store the day's three** so a reload cannot differ — rejected: the seeded shuffle gives the same property with nothing to store, expire or lose, and a stored pick is a second place for the pool to be wrong.
- **Rotate with a cursor through the pool** — rejected: a cursor is state, it changes the answer to *what comes next* when a Question leaves the pool, and it makes the page depend on how often it was opened.
- **Report every changed file from `mtime`** — the brief's literal *recent vault activity*. Rejected for decision 7.
- **Group by free-text topic names, as prototype 08 does** (*consolidation vs encoding*) — no mechanism in the app produces them. Headings are Tags as written.
- **Roll Tags up to a chosen depth on Home** — rejected for decision 9.

## What this moves in the prototypes

`08-home.html` is not re-pinned (ADR 0032 rejected it). Beside the departures ADR 0032 records, a reader of the PNG should know: the group headings are Tags as written and not the prototype's topic names; *Hypothesis falsified — run-2026-09-14, F1 met* is not an item (decision 8); and the prototype's dashboards line shows a count beside Loose Ends, which the brief rules out.
