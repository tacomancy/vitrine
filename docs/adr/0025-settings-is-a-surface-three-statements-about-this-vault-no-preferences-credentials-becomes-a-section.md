# 0025: Settings is a surface — three statements about this vault, no preferences, and Credentials becomes a section of it

**Status:** Accepted

The rail has carried a gear since the first sketch — `00-shape` draws seven glyphs, six surfaces and then `⚙` at `margin-top:auto`, set apart at the bottom — and no prototype has ever drawn what it opens. ADR 0017 decision 3 rejected the surface behind it as "a ninth surface the brief never lists, **holding one thing**", and put the Credentials panel on two doors instead: the app menu, and the Scout form's *Add a key*. Story KEEP-12 ([tacomancy.com/vitrine_stories](https://tacomancy.com/vitrine_stories/)) asks for each provider's key "set, replaced, or removed from **one place**", and the tree's own resolution of *Configuration has no owner* puts three things under Keep — where the vault is (KEEP-10), where the PDFs are (KEEP-11), what it talks to (KEEP-12). Two doors onto one thing is not a place, and the second half of decision 3's objection no longer holds. The call was made on [#253](https://github.com/tacomancy/vitrine/issues/253) on 2026-09-26; this ADR is the grill that scoped it. The shape: **a ninth screen that is not one of the eight Surfaces, holding statements about how this vault is arranged and no preference whatever**; the Credentials panel becomes a **section** of it and the app-menu door goes; the PDF arrangement is **shown and never set**, which is what makes "Vitrine never moves a byte" a property of the API rather than a promise. ADR 0017 decisions 1, 2 and 4–11 are untouched, and its dated update points here.

## Decisions

1. **Settings is a ninth screen, and it is not one of the eight Surfaces.** `CONTEXT.md` already sorts screens by what they are for: **Surface** is the brief's eight, **Dashboard** is the three that read the work, **First run** is explicitly not a Surface and **Ingest review** is a panel. Settings is about neither the work nor a reading of it but the arrangement it sits in, so it earns its own entry rather than growing a list the brief fixed. The gear's position in `00-shape` — below the six, separated by `margin-top:auto` — is the same distinction drawn a year earlier, and naming it in the glossary is what stops a future beat from filing a ninth *research* surface behind the same precedent. Renumbering the eight was rejected: a reader who finds "nine surfaces" in `CONTEXT.md` and eight in the brief has to work out which list is wrong.
2. **Settings holds statements about how this vault is arranged — each a fact with a referent outside the app, checkable against the world. It holds no preference: no value whose only effect is on the app's own behaviour.** This is the rule, not the number three. A surface defended by a count is re-litigated by the first ticket that needs a global default; a surface defended by a rule has a test that ticket can apply. Under it the three are the three, and the values the brief leaves configurable stay where they are: the artifact size threshold (KEEP-9, beat 4) is a default with a per-artifact override, the backward-search backstop (ADR 0016) is a default with a per-search override, and Skim's cutoff, per-run caps and the Queue's aging window are the Scout's or the Queue's. There are no themes, no fonts, no sync toggle, no account, no notifications. Prompt 13's stated failure mode is that this becomes a preferences window; the rule is what it is defended with.
3. **The Credentials panel becomes a section of Settings, and the app-menu door goes.** ADR 0017 decision 3's panel is unchanged in what it does — a block per Provider, a key field that never echoes the stored value, an editable model id, *test*, *remove* — and changed only in where it is drawn. The app-menu item is removed: it was a door onto something with no place. The Scout form keeps its *Add a key*, now a `pushRoute` to `#/settings`, so back returns to the form (ADR 0021 decision 2). Two renderings of one panel was rejected — that is decision 3's own objection restated as an implementation. **The cost is named:** the form's values are client state and the round trip must not lose them, so the Scout form's ticket owns keeping them across the navigation.
4. **One block per Provider, and no *add a provider*.** The section's shape is per-Provider and today there is exactly one, so it draws one block headed *Anthropic*. A Provider is added by shipping an implementation beside the first (ADR 0017 decision 1), not by a user typing a name, and a control implying otherwise would ship the untested provider picker that decision rejected. KEEP-12's "each provider's key… from one place" is met literally; a second implementation is a second block and no new chrome.
5. **Settings has an Address, `#/settings`, and ⌘, opens it; the gear is layout; First run does not reach it.** Reachability is ADR 0021's one rule — has an Address, reached by the Global command — and this surface is no exception. Whether the Sidebar draws the gear, where, and in which form stays layout the prototypes win on (ADR 0021 decision 3), so this ADR records `00-shape`'s gear without pinning it. ⌘, is decided here rather than left to layout because it is an OS convention, not a choice: a Mac app that swallows it is surprising. It does not collide with beat 2b's open question of which of ⌘K and `⌘'` survives. **From First run there is no way in:** that screen has one action and must not read as a wizard (`CONTEXT.md` § First run), and two of the three sections have nothing to say with no vault open.
6. **The PDF arrangement is shown and never set, and no procedure the app exposes can change it.** The section states four facts, which is exactly enough to check the arrangement: the path, whether it resolves and *to what* — the symlink's target spelled out in full — how many PDFs are there, and when one last arrived. There is no control that creates, repoints or removes the symlink, and none that copies a PDF into or out of the folder. This is decision 4 of ADR 0017 in the other register: as "no `credentials.get`" makes BYOK a property of the API, an absent write path makes **"whatever already syncs the researcher's files syncs it; Vitrine never moves a byte"** (`docs/architecture.md` § Decided, story KEEP-11) enforceable by shape rather than by discipline. A *choose PDF folder* button was rejected: it is the sync setting the architecture says does not exist. A symlink that does not resolve is the one thing on this surface that is genuinely *broken*, and is drawn as a fault — never as an empty folder.
7. ***Open a different vault* is the same pick, and there is no recent-vaults list.** The control calls `vault.pick`, which First run already uses; on success the route resets to the Inbox, the queries invalidate, and the watcher and index re-point — a fresh start on the new vault, not a relaunch, and nothing on disk is touched on either side. Beside it: the path, *reveal in Finder*, and whether the watcher is healthy, which is a fact about the arrangement and so belongs here — but the *alarm* stays in the footer channel where **Not watching** already lives, and Settings never draws it a second time. A recent-vaults list was rejected under decision 2: it is a convenience with no referent in the world, and the macOS chooser already reopens where the user was.
8. ***Blocked on credentials* is visible from this side as named Scouts, never as a count.** From a Scout's row the state is health derived from run rows (ADR 0016 decision 10, ADR 0017 decision 7); from here the fact is about the key — *no key stored*, *a key is stored*, or *key rejected*, the last from the most recent `credentials` run or from *test*. The section also names the Scouts currently waiting — *Born lab*, *Interpretability sweep* — each a link to its row. That is what makes the state legible from this side: an absence here has a consequence there, which a single Scout's row can only ever show one instance of. A number was rejected: story HOLD-5 rules out a total and the app draws no badges, while names are not a total and each one leads somewhere. Derived from the same run rows as everywhere else, so Home, Loose Ends, Scout Activity and Settings cannot disagree. A locked Keychain or a denied ACL dialog stays a fault shown as one, never folded into either state (ADR 0017 decision 3).
9. **Settings is where honest-empty is easiest, and it is the reference the other surfaces are drawn against rather than an exception to them.** Every section has a sentence to say when the answer is nothing — *nothing has arrived yet* is a statement about the world, not a blank list — so this surface is never empty on the freshest possible vault. That makes it the clearest case of story KEEP-10's rule, not a screen exempt from it: whatever carries honest-empty on the four screens Prompt 12 draws should be recognisably the same thing that carries it here. Its fresh-vault state is therefore not a fourth state to invent but the same three sections making their emptiest true statements.
10. **The surface lands in two beats: a new beat 4b for the shell, the vault section and the PDF section; beat 7 adds Credentials to a surface that already exists.** 4b sits immediately before beat 5 because Ingest is the first thing that silently depends on the PDF arrangement — a paper that never arrived because a symlink broke is precisely the confusion this surface prevents, and shipping it after Ingest means the beat that most needs it is built without it. An early slot on the beat 2b model was rejected: beats 3 and 4 touch nothing here, so there is no retrofit to avoid. The whole surface in beat 7 was rejected: it leaves KEEP-10 and KEEP-11 unanswered for most of the roadmap. **The costs:** two specs against one prototype, and a Settings that is two-thirds of itself through beats 5 and 6 — honestly incomplete rather than broken, provided it draws no Credentials heading it cannot fill.

## Considered options

- **A ninth *Surface*, growing `CONTEXT.md`'s list to nine.** Rejected, decision 1.
- **"Three things and no more" as a count.** Rejected, decision 2: the first global default breaks it and re-opens the surface.
- **The Credentials panel kept as a panel, drawn in Settings and also opened in place over the Scout form.** Rejected, decision 3 — two renderings of one thing is ADR 0017 decision 3's own objection.
- **An *add a provider* control.** Rejected, decision 4.
- **Pinning the rail's gear as the way in.** Rejected, decision 5: that is layout, and ADR 0021 decision 3 leaves it to the prototypes.
- **Settings reachable from First run.** Rejected, decision 5.
- **A *choose PDF folder* control, or an offer to make the symlink.** Rejected, decision 6: Vitrine arranging the sync it promised not to arrange.
- **A recent-vaults list.** Rejected, decision 7.
- **A count of blocked Scouts.** Rejected, decision 8 (HOLD-5).
- **The whole surface in beat 7; a beat 2c before beat 3.** Rejected, decision 10.
- **Deciding the vault and PDF procedures' signatures here.** Rejected: no secret rides on them, so pinning a shape in an ADR is over-deciding. What the surface must be able to show is decisions 6 and 7; the procedures are `docs/architecture.md` § Settings. The one exception is the guarantee in decision 6, which is the *absence* of a procedure and so cannot be left to a spec.
- **An `## Update` on ADR 0017 carrying all of this.** Rejected: a reader looking for why the app spells out a symlink's target would never find it in an ADR titled for BYOK. 0017 gets a dated update naming what changed in it; the scope lives here.

## Consequences

- **+** KEEP-12's "one place" is literal, and it is the only place: no app-menu door, no second rendering, one section that a second Provider extends without new chrome.
- **+** "Vitrine never moves a byte" gains the same kind of proof BYOK has. The surface that would host a sync setting is the surface that documents there is none, and has no procedure that could add one.
- **+** *Blocked on credentials* can now be read from both ends — the Scout that is waiting, and the key it is waiting on — from one derivation, so the two can never disagree.
- **+** The three configurable values the brief leaves loose (artifact threshold, backstop, Skim cutoff) now have a stated reason to live where they live, instead of being homeless by omission.
- **−** Nine screens, and a glossary that has to explain why the ninth is not one of the eight. Decision 1 accepts the explanation as the cheaper of the two confusions.
- **−** Two beats build one surface, so the prototype is drawn once and read twice, and 4b's spec has to say what beat 7 will add without building a placeholder for it.
- **−** The Scout form's *Add a key* now costs a navigation, and the form has to survive it. A regression here is silent — a half-filled form lost on the way to set a key — so the ticket that adds the link owns the test.
- **−** `CONTEXT.md` **Surface** and **Blocked on credentials** both widen, and a new **Settings** term joins them.
- **−** Beat 4b's spec is blocked on the Settings prototype (#253), which is drawn by hand in Claude Design and pinned to the frozen tier. Prompt 13 lives on the beat issue, since `docs/reference/design-prompts.md` is frozen at eleven — the same arrangement ADR 0021's update made for beat 2b.
