# 0041: The Question Map is one page — the matrix leads, it cuts itself rather than change shape, and inferred links are offered, never drawn

**Status:** Accepted

The brief's Question Map is "four panels sharing a name" (§ Open questions), and prototype 11 stacks them. Beat 8 (#175) ships the first of them. Ticket #91 is the one ADR for this page: it records what is cut, what is kept, and the layout. Three departures raised against the prototype — the backlog note (#252), the ranked-list fallback (#245), and the Map's grammar for machine proposals (#255) — each said "carry this to #91's ADR", and so does the funnel's cut (#92). This ADR is from a `grill-with-docs` pass on 2026-10-05. It is the single record; it does not reopen the three it carries.

## Decisions

1. **One page, the coverage matrix leads, and beat 8 draws only what exists.** The attention scatter (beat 10) joins the page as a visibly separate second panel with its own *as of* line, never merged with the matrix — two data sources, two pictures. A fourth Dashboard was rejected: it breaks the brief's three-Dashboard symmetry to solve a layout problem that disappears once the funnel is gone. Beat 8 draws no placeholder for the scatter; the Sidebar's hollow-entry rule (ADR 0032) already says what is not built. Revisited when beat 10's spec is written, which may find the scatter wants a page of its own — the page's layout is cheap to change until then.
2. **The flow funnel is cut, not relocated; Origins survives as a reading** (#92, the reasoning is its resolution comment). Origins sits beside the matrix as a list, not a panel. It groups Provenance by the `from` target across every Status and every context, with *unattached* captures as one line named so. Each row carries two facts — how many Questions it produced, and how much Material is attached to them — so a high first number over a thin second one names a source worth going back to. Ranked by Questions produced and cut to the top 10 with the cut stated; the full list opens on demand. The prototype's *read 3×* and *41 sessions* are accumulation and do not ship.
3. **The matrix truncates in place and never changes shape** (#245). 24 rows × 22 columns, code constants; ties break by recency for rows (the later of `captured` and `promoted`) and by Tag name for columns. The cut is stated on the page in the prototype's own line — *64 questions · 44 tags · matrix shows the 24 × 22 heaviest* — and the clause is dropped when nothing is cut. The ranked-list fallback past ~40 questions is dropped: the cut comes off the top of the sort, so the empty rows are exactly what falls away.
4. **Unanchored is read from the four readings, not from the matrix's bottom.** Well-supported, unanchored, unquestioned knowledge, and clocked but unquestioned are each a count per kind naming a decision — the shape Loose Ends already allows (#252), never summed. A reading with no members collapses; one with members opens to its full ranked list on demand, never automatically and never because of a size. When the page cannot prove it looked — the Index not Current, the vault not read in full, the watcher down — the strip says *not yet* in the footer channel's words instead, since a collapsed reading would be a Claim it cannot warrant (ADR 0032).
5. **Coverage counts distinct Material, not cells.** A cell is the Material attached to a Map row that carries the Tag; a row's weight is its distinct Material; a column's weight the distinct Material carrying the Tag across the rows shown. A highlight takes its Source's Tags and counts once with its Source. Summing cells would count a paper with three Tags three times and make a well-tagged paper look like a well-supported Question.
6. **A Map row is one open thread of inquiry.** An open Question not yet promoted, or an open Research Question with its originating Question folded in, so a thread is never split across two rows. Answered and abandoned threads are not rows, nor is a Hypothesis, whose edges are Evidence. No age threshold makes a row unanchored: age is a neutral, sortable fact.
7. **Clocked but unquestioned is derived, so it says *kept from a Scout*.** A stub whose `origin_scout` is set, whose Scout was Assigned to no Question, with no Related edge since — counted per Tag. A stub records no Lane, and recording one only to name this reading was rejected. The wording diverges from the brief's *Skim items*, deliberately: a Review-lane card from an unassigned Scout is the same evidence of an unarticulated interest.
8. **One depth control, owned by the page.** The matrix's columns follow it, and the scatter obeys it when it arrives; it defaults to the deepest Tags in use, so a parent and its own children never sit side by side, double-counting. Implicit Tags appear only when rolled up to.
9. **Inferred links are offered as Candidate links, and accepting writes Related.** A candidate is a (Question, paper) pair sharing at least one Tag with an unanchored row, ranked by shared Tags then recency; already-linked papers and rejected pairs never appear. Keyword overlap needs the Lexicon and waits for beat 10, on the same terms ADR 0016 put accept history on a wait list: a placeholder match would teach a wrong order. Accept writes a Related edge through `questions.link` and never Supporting or Opposing — attaching is the judgement (ADR 0020), and prototype 11's *unsorted* column is the side that ADR rejected.
10. **The review inherits ADR 0016's grammar and does not reopen it** (#255): accept `A`, open `O`, pass `P`, reject `R`. Pass records nothing and returns the candidate to the bottom of the session's stack. Reject is written to `.vitrine/dismissals.json` per pair — subject the Question's `id`, row kind `inferred-link:<paper id>` — so rejecting one paper never silences another, and is undoable while the candidate is on screen; accept is not, because it wrote a file (the Queue's rule, ADR 0039). Entered from an unanchored row; the Question with the most candidates first, then newest. There is no *1 of N* and no backlog note: after a Question's last candidate is decided or passed the review offers *next question* and nothing more (#252). `j`/`k` moves focus over the candidates through the id the list publishes (ADR 0030).
11. **The matrix is an interface, not a picture.** A row label opens its Question or Research Question; a column header names the Tag and stops until the Vault surface gives a Tag an Address; a cell opens an inline list of its Material, each item opening where it has an Address. It is one `role="grid"` under ADR 0030's chosen-id rule, with an accessible label per cell (*"<question> · <tag>: <n> items"*), a legend that states its bin edges, and the exact count on focus — intensity is never colour alone.
12. **Chart colour is bound by class, and the lint rule lands with this chart** (#141). Bins are `none · 1 · 2–3 · 4–7 · 8+`, class names `none` and `b1…b4`, bound in the matrix's CSS Module to `--color-seq-5`, `-4`, `-2`, `-1`; `none` is the surface token. Contrast is checked in all three theme states by hidden-run captures. The no-colour-literal rule and its `fill="var(--color-<ramp>-*)"` RuleTester case are the first ticket, so the matrix is written under them.
13. **The Map says what it could not read.** A Partial or Unreadable Question is never a row (ADR 0009); the Map carries the Inbox's quiet footer line counting them and opening to each path and reason. An edge counts only if its link resolves: a Related entry that lands on nothing contributes no Material, and the row says *a link in this question's Related lands on nothing* rather than reading as plainly unanchored.
14. **Short pages are Claims with Warrants** (ADR 0032). No open Questions: *"No open questions to map."* warranted by *read 0 of 0 questions · just now*. Questions but no Material: the unanchored reading leads, and the matrix area says it has no columns, warranted by *N open questions read · 0 sources attached*. A grid with no axes is never drawn.
15. **Beat 8 adds no Loose Ends row and no Home item.** The brief's boundary holds — "unquestioned knowledge" is analytical and stays on the Map; an orphan note is a defect and lives on Loose Ends. Unanchored and the other readings are not defects, so they never become rows.

**Update 2026-10-05 (#483).** Decision 8's roll-up rule, stated where two tickets would otherwise each guess it: a Tag at depth *d* is its first *d* segments, and a Tag already shallower stays as it is. At the default depth — the deepest Tag in use — a parent whose children are also in use is its own column holding only what is tagged exactly that, so a parent and its children never double-count; at a shallower depth the parent is the union of its own and its children's, and an Implicit parent appears only then, as exactly that union. Every reading on the page follows the depth the page is at, not only the matrix's columns: the four readings group by the same rolled-up Tags. A depth outside what is in use is clamped to it.

## Considered options

- **The scatter as its own Dashboard.** Rejected, decision 1: breaks the three-Dashboard symmetry, and nothing in beat 8 forces it.
- **Keeping the funnel, or a status-count row without a time axis.** Rejected by #92: a badge in disguise.
- **A ranked-list fallback past ~40 questions.** Rejected, decision 3 (#245).
- **A backlog note and a *1 of N* count on the review.** Rejected, decision 10 (#252).
- **Cell weight as a sum of its cells.** Rejected, decision 5.
- **Recording a Lane on the stub to read *from Skim*.** Rejected, decision 7.
- **Accept writing the paper onto the Supporting side as *unsorted*.** Rejected, decision 9 (ADR 0020).
- **A keyword-overlap candidate before the Lexicon exists.** Rejected, decision 9.
- **One dismissal per Question for all its candidates.** Rejected, decision 10: a decline of one paper would silence the rest.
- **A recorded session for pass.** Rejected, decision 10: pass records nothing, and persisting it would silence a candidate by inaction (#255).

## Consequences

- Beat 8's spec answers #175's question: no Loose Ends rows, no Home items (decision 15), and the empty states are decision 14.
- `CONTEXT.md` gains Material, Map row, the four readings, Candidate link, and a tightened Origins and Coverage.
- `docs/architecture.md` gains § Question Map; `dismissals.json`'s entry in § Vault layout now covers declined candidates, which its own line already named.
- The prototype's panel 2 (scatter) and panels 3–4 (funnel and origins) are read as above: scatter deferred to beat 10, origins kept as a list, funnel cut. Prototype 11 is not re-pinned, so there is no site issue.
- Reversibility, hardest first: the `dismissals.json` key shape (a persisted format), what accept writes into the user's files, the definitions other surfaces will read (Material, Map row, Origins' scope), the lint rule's class-name convention, then layout and constants.

**Update 2026-10-05 (#485).** Where the readings' definitions needed a number or a unit, stated once so no later ticket guesses: *well-supported* is the five Map rows with most Material (`WELL_SUPPORTED_ROWS`, a code constant), never a row with none — the only reading whose list is cut, because "the top of the weight sort" has no meaning uncut; its count is the rows shown. *Unquestioned knowledge* is read by Tag, as the brief reads it: a Tag no Map row's Material carries, counted in Tags and ranked by the Material under it. *Clocked but unquestioned* is counted in stubs, each once however many Tags it has, and listed per Tag ranked by stubs under it; "assigned" is read from the stub's own `origin_question` (§ Scouts carries the assignment onto it at acceptance), and "linked since" is any resolved link from a Question or Research Question file. The default depth is the deepest Tag in use by any Source or stub, attached or not, so the readings and the matrix sit at one depth.

**Update 2026-10-05 (#489).** Accept writes the Related edge where the row's Kind keeps it: `questions.link` for a Question, and for a Research Question one line under `## Related questions` (`researchQuestions.link`), refusing a section that is missing or present twice. Candidates cover both kinds of row.
