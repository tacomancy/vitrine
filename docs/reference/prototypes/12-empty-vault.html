VITRINE · THE EMPTY VAULT
Short because nothing is there — never because something didn't load.

One slot, three voices. Every surface that can be empty has a first row, where its contents would begin. What sits in that row is always one of three things, and they differ in typeface, in completeness, and in whether the foot of the window speaks. Learn them on the Inbox and they read the same on any surface drawn after it.

1 — FIRST LAUNCH, NO VAULT OPEN

Seen once: one sentence and one action. The macOS picker can already make a new folder, so one button handles both cases, and the note beside it says so. Focus starts on the button (brass ring), so ↵ opens the picker. There's no rail, no ⌘K and no capture line, because none of them works yet, and the screen doesn't hint that they exist.

2 — QUESTION INBOX, NOTHING CAPTURED

The columns stay drawn. Question · where it came from · age is the plainest description of what lands here. The claim says captured rather than left: it describes where questions come from, not how many are done, so the empty list can't be read as finished. The paragraph below covers how questions arrive, once, at the page's own size. There are no counts, and nothing reads as all clear.

3 — LOOSE ENDS, NOTHING LOOSE

This keeps that the app can see, because that clause sets the limit of the claim. It drops yet, which sounded like a promise; the honest-empty weight moves into the warrant, where every surface carries it. The paragraph describes scope, not a checklist: it says what this page looks for, so the empty page still reads as the maintenance surface and not as a page that failed to render.

An inert entry keeps its place and its label, switches to a hollow glyph, and says where its contents will come from. The rail stays a map of the product. Waiting is shown as provenance still to come, not by greying the entry out, which is what makes an entry look broken. Home, Inbox, Scout Queue, Vault, Loose Ends and Settings stay live, because each has something to do on day one. Saying an entry has nothing is a claim, so it follows the same rule as the empty line: while the vault is being read, or while it can't be watched, every entry is drawn live and the rail claims nothing.
